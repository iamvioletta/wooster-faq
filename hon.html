What's not included in the project?

We do not include the purchase of your domain name, stock art, font licenses, media, printing costs, hosting, or premium plugins. If your site would benefit from stock imagery, you can purchase a stock image package. We can also recommend a brand photographer for you.

Who provides the website photos?

The client is responsible for providing photos for the website. We can recommend photographers or stock imagery.

Do you build accessible websites?

Our custom websites are built to WCAG 2.1 AA standards with ADA compliance in mind. We care deeply about web accessibility. Some accessibility improvements may qualify for tax credits or deductions. Please talk to your accountant for details.

What about hosting & web maintenance?

After a web project launches, we offer ongoing site maintenance and hosting. The cost is quoted per individual project.

How do you handle project management?

We use an app called Asana to organize our tasks, due dates, and conversations. When we start the project, we'll send you an Asana invite.
